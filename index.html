About the project

Every forest begins with someone who decides to plant.

Vruksha means tree in Sanskrit. It is also the name we gave to our promise: to put living roots into the ground, and to do it together.

Carried out by the Leo Club of Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka for the Leo Tree Plantation Challenge – Season 7, Vruksha brought our Leos out of the lecture halls and into the red earth, with saplings in hand and mammoties on shoulders.

What we planted is small today. That is exactly the point.

Our journey

From a seed of an idea to roots in the ground

Scroll and watch it grow.

  2. 🌰
    Stage 01

    The seed

    It started with a plan and a committee. Leads, coordinators, designers, writers and a media team came together to shape Vruksha, our club's entry to the Leo Tree Plantation Challenge.

  3. 🌱
    Stage 02

    The saplings

    Healthy young plants were chosen and brought in, each one in its nursery bag, each one waiting for a permanent home.

  4. ⛏️
    Stage 03

    Breaking ground

    Before anything can grow, someone has to dig. Our Leos took turns with the mammoty, opening the red soil pit by pit, with plenty of laughter along the way.

  5. 🤲
    Stage 04

    Planting, hand in hand

    One sapling, many hands. Each tree was set into the earth and pressed in gently by the people who will come back to watch it grow.

  6. 🌳
    Stage 05

    Growing together

    The day ended with soil on our hands and a line of new trees behind us. The planting is done. The growing has just begun.
